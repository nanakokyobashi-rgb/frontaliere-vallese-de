Vollständige Beschreibung

Join us in shaping the Quality organization at our Vionnaz site. As Head of Quality Assurance Site Vionnaz & Responsible Person (a), 100%, you will provide leadership for the site Quality Assurance organization and ensure an effective, compliant and risk-based Pharmaceutical Quality System. In this key leadership role, you will act as Responsible Person (RP) for the Vionnaz site and provide quality oversight across GMP operations, product quality, batch disposition and release-related activities, deviations, CAPA, change control, quality risk management and other critical quality processes. You will be a member of the site leadership team and work closely with Manufacturing, QC, Supply Chain, Engineering and global Quality functions to ensure compliance, patient safety, reliable supply and continuous improvement.

Your tasks

  • Lead and develop the Quality Assurance organization at the Vionnaz site, ensuring clear responsibilities, strong collaboration and a culture of quality and accountability.
  • Act as Responsible Person (RP) for the Vionnaz site in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and internal procedures.
  • Ensure an effective and compliant Pharmaceutical Quality System, including deviations, investigations, CAPA, change control, document management, training and quality risk management.
  • Provide senior Quality oversight for critical GMP activities and decisions, including manufacturing, validation, qualification, technology transfer, supply chain, batch release, major deviations, significant changes, Quality Agreements, product quality issues and complaints.
  • Ensure inspection and audit readiness and lead Quality interactions with regulatory authorities, customers and external auditors.
  • Represent Quality within the Vionnaz site leadership team and contribute to site strategy, priorities and business decisions.
  • Work with global Quality functions to translate global standards and regulatory requirements into effective site processes and support harmonization across Bachem.
  • Define and monitor Quality KPIs and drive sustainable improvements in compliance, efficiency and right-first-time performance.
  • Build and develop a high-performing QA team through clear accountabilities, capability building, coaching and talent development.

Your profile

  • Master's degree or PhD in Pharmacy, Chemistry, Life Sciences, Biotechnology, or a related scientific discipline.
  • Qualification and experience required to act as Responsible Person (RP) in Switzerland.
  • Minimum 10 years of experience in the pharmaceutical, biotech, or CDMO industry, including leadership experience in GMP-regulated manufacturing environments.
  • Strong expertise in Pharmaceutical Quality Systems and GMP regulations (Swiss, EU, and US), including inspection and audit management.
  • Proven ability to lead complex quality organizations, manage critical quality events, and make risk-based decisions.
  • Understanding of manufacturing, validation, qualification, technology transfer, and batch release processes.
  • Strong leadership, stakeholder management, and communication skills.
  • Excellent French and English language skills.
  • Experience with GMP-relevant digital systems such as MasterControl, Veeva, LIMS, and SAP is an advantage.

Our offer

  • A modern and dynamic working environment within a fast-growing international company.
  • Challenging projects of high scientific and technical value.
  • Opportunities for professional development and continuing professional development.
  • A corporate culture based on collaboration, innovation and excellence.

Attractive working conditions and competitive employee benefits.
